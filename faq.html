Frequently asked questions

Straight answers, no fine print. Still unsure? Call 204-972-0325.

How much does gutter cleaning cost?

$1.75 per linear foot with a $100 minimum charge. So a smaller home lands near the base fee and a larger home pays per foot. Three-story homes run $2.63 per foot because of the extra reach and setup. Every job gets an exact written quote before we start, and the instant estimate tool runs the same math.

What does a downspout flush cost?

$15 per downspout. We unclog the elbow and flush the full run so water moves freely again. Clogged downspouts are the number one cause of gutter overflow.

How much is guard installation?

$8.50 per linear foot, including fitting. Guards keep leaves and debris out so the gutters stay clear much longer.

Are inspections really free?

Yes. We come out, look at the gutters, and give you an exact written quote. No obligation. On-site visits are weekdays at 12:30. Book one here.

What deposit do you take?

30% to book the job, balance on completion. No tax.

Where do you work?

Winnipeg with no travel fee, plus Steinbach ($45 travel fee), Grunthal ($55), Selkirk, Stonewall and surrounding communities. Not sure if we cover you? Just ask.

Why does three-story cleaning cost more?

The extra reach and setup takes longer and needs more equipment. The per-foot rate is 50% higher, which works out to $2.63/ft.

How often should gutters be cleaned?

In Winnipeg, twice a year is the safe rule: once after the spring melt and once after the fall leaves. Homes under heavy tree cover may need a third pass.

Do you repair gutters, or only clean them?

Both. We re-secure sagging sections and replace broken hangers and clips, and for systems past saving we do full replacement, measured and fitted.

How do I book?

Call or text 204-972-0325, email guttergeniewpg@gmail.com, or use the quote form. Fastest response is by phone.
