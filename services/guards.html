Gutter guard installation

Gutter guards sit over the trough and let water in while keeping leaves, needles and seed pods out. They do not make gutters maintenance-free, small grit still settles, but they turn a twice-a-year cleanout into an occasional check. For homes under heavy tree cover, they pay for themselves fast.

Who they suit best

  • Homes under mature trees, especially elm, maple and pine
  • Anyone tired of paying for two cleanouts a year
  • Properties where ladder work is awkward or unsafe
  • New or recently replaced gutter systems worth protecting

How we install

  1. Inspect and clean first. Guards go on clean, sound gutters. We fix any loose hangers or bad slope before installing.
  2. Measure and cut to fit. Guards are fitted to your actual runs, around corners and downspout drops, not generic snap-in strips.
  3. Secure properly. Fastened so they stay put through Winnipeg freeze-thaw and spring runoff.
  4. Test the flow. Water run-through confirms drainage before we call it done.

Pricing

$8.50 per linear foot, fitting included. The gutters are cleaned as part of the job if they need it. No tax. 30% deposit to book, balance on completion.

Honest note

Guards dramatically reduce debris but they are not magic. Fine grit and pine needles can still work through over the years. Plan on a quick check every year or two, which is a fraction of the cost of regular full cleanouts.
