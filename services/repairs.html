Hanger & clip repair

Gutters fail slowly. Hangers loosen, clips snap, sections sag and pull away from the fascia. Left alone, a sagging section holds standing water, which rots the fascia and ices up in winter. Catching it early is a small repair. Ignoring it is a replacement.

What we fix

  • Sagging or drooping sections that hold standing water
  • Broken or missing hangers and clips
  • Gutters pulling away from the fascia board
  • Disconnected or damaged downspouts
  • Leaking seams and end caps
  • Sections with the wrong slope, draining the wrong way

How a repair goes

  1. Full walkthrough. We check every hanger, seam and downspout connection, not just the spot you noticed.
  2. Written quote. You get the exact price before any work starts. No surprises.
  3. Re-secure and replace. Old hangers out, new ones in, sections re-pitched so water actually runs to the downspout.
  4. Water test. We run water through the repaired sections to prove the slope is right.

Pricing

Repair pricing depends on what is wrong, which is exactly why the inspection is free. Most hanger and clip repairs are quoted on the spot at the visit. Cleaning can be added at the standard rates ($1.75/ft, $100 minimum). No tax. 30% deposit to book, balance on completion.

Repair or replace?

We will tell you straight. If a section has one bad hanger run, repair it. If the whole system is rusted through, full of holes, or sagging everywhere, replacement is cheaper than chasing repairs. See gutter replacement for the full story.
