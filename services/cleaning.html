Gutter cleaning

Leaves, needles, shingle grit and sludge pack into gutters a little more every season. Once the trough is full, water has nowhere to go but over the edge, down the siding and into the ground next to your foundation. A proper cleanout puts the whole system back to working order.

Signs yours are due

  • Water pouring over the edge during rain instead of out the downspouts
  • Plants or grass growing in the trough (more common than people admit)
  • Gutters pulling away from the fascia under the weight of wet debris
  • Basement dampness or pooling near the foundation after storms
  • It has been more than a year since the last cleanout

How we clean

  1. Walk the roofline. We look at every run, check hangers and downspouts, and note problem spots before touching anything.
  2. Scoop and bag the debris. Everything comes out by hand, not just blown further down the line. Bagged and taken away.
  3. Check every downspout. If one is clogged we clear it, a clogged downspout makes a clean gutter useless.
  4. Flush and test. Water through the system to confirm flow, then a final check for leaks at seams and corners.

How often?

In Winnipeg, twice a year is the safe schedule: once after the spring melt and once after the fall leaves drop. Homes under heavy tree cover may need a third pass mid-summer.
