Downspout clearing & flushing

Here is the thing most people miss: the gutters can be spotless and still overflow if the downspouts are clogged. Leaves, tennis balls, and packed shingle grit collect at the elbows and in underground runs. One clogged downspout turns its whole gutter section into a waterfall over your siding.

Signs a downspout is clogged

  • Water barely trickles out the bottom during rain while the gutter overflows above
  • Gurgling or dripping sounds from inside the downspout after rain stops
  • Visible bulging at an elbow, or a seam that has popped open
  • Water pooling at the foundation directly below a downspout

How we clear them

  1. Inspect top to bottom. We check the drop outlet, each elbow, and the ground-level exit for blockages.
  2. Break up the clog. Blockages are worked loose from the elbows and joints, the usual choke points.
  3. Flush the full run. High-flow water from the top confirms the whole pipe is clear, not just the first elbow.
  4. Check the exit. We confirm water discharges away from the house, and re-secure any loose straps.
